Feed a Goose

Feed a Goose is and was my introductory project to web developement. I wanted to start teaching myself javascript and html and css to develop my front end capacities as I truly enjoy the the visual design aspect of web developement - most of my career has been focused on back end developement... Also, I've never even made a website before so I really had a lot to learn on this journey. At first, I wasn't really sure what kind of website I wanted to build, what it would look like, nothing really. Just I knew i wanted to learn. I didn't really put much thought into it at the beginning, I randomly thought it would be kind of funny to have a live webcam of my cat placed somewhere where people would be able to watch him. This is where the seed was planted. At first, The website was nothing more that a white page, with a video playing in the middle that served as a place holder for the camera once I could get the proper equipment. There was a title to the page, and that was that. It was a pretty shit website if you ask me. However, soon enough the site began to have this snowball effect. I wanted to keep adding new functionalities to the website.
